# Equipment Maintenance and Calibration SOP

## 2.1 Calibration Interval

Life-support and infusion equipment must be calibrated at intervals defined by the manufacturer, and no less frequently than every 12 months.

## 2.2 Out-of-Service Tagging

Equipment that fails calibration or is suspected faulty must be tagged out of service immediately and removed from the clinical area within 1 hour.

## 2.3 Responsible Role

Biomedical Engineering performs calibration; the unit charge nurse ensures faulty equipment is not reused before it is tagged.
