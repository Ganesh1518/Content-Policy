# Critical Lab Value Notification SOP

## 3.1 Critical Value Definition

A critical value is any laboratory result that falls outside the facility-defined critical range (e.g., serum potassium below 2.5 mmol/L or above 6.5 mmol/L) and requires immediate clinical action.

## 3.2 Notification Timeline

The laboratory must notify the ordering clinician or covering nurse within 30 minutes of a critical value being verified.

## 3.3 Read-Back Requirement

The receiving clinician must read back the value and the patient identifier to the laboratory technologist before the call ends.

## 3.4 Responsible Role

The laboratory technologist initiates notification; the receiving nurse documents the read-back and timestamp in the chart.
