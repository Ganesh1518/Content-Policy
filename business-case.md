# Business Case — Hospital Policy & SOP Assistant

**Business Case ID:** AAIE_011_HLC · **Domain:** Healthcare — Hospital Operations & Compliance

## 1. Problem

Hospital staff need fast, accurate answers about operational policies and
procedures — infection control, patient-safety protocols, consent
requirements, and compliance obligations — grounded strictly in the
hospital's own (synthetic, for this project) policy manuals and SOPs.
Ungrounded or hallucinated answers in this domain carry real operational
risk, so every answer must be traceable to a specific clause, and the
assistant must refuse to answer when the corpus does not support a
confident response, rather than guessing.

## 2. Target Users

| User | Need |
|---|---|
| Bedside nurses | Quick lookup of timing, sequencing, and documentation requirements during a shift (e.g., "how often must a fall-risk score be recalculated?"). |
| Charge nurses / house supervisors | Escalation thresholds and responsible-role lookups when a policy exception occurs. |
| Compliance / Infection Control officers | Fast retrieval of exact clause wording to support audits and incident reviews. |
| New or agency staff | Onboarding reference for facility-specific procedures without waiting on a colleague. |

## 3. Corpus Description

A **synthetic** corpus of 30 documents under `data/corpus/`, generated by
`scripts/generate_corpus.py`, covering five categories:

| Category | Documents | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Infection control | 5 | Hand Hygiene Policy, PPE Donning/Doffing SOP, Isolation Precautions SOP |
| Patient safety protocols | 6 | Patient Identification, Fall Risk, Medication Safety, Surgical Time-Out |
| Consent procedures | 5 | Surgical Consent, Minors/Incapacitated Consent, Blood Product Consent |
| Compliance manual | 6 | Incident Management, PHI Handling, Controlled Substances, Credentialing |
| Operational policies | 8 | Admission/Bed Assignment, Staffing Ratios, Code Blue Response |

Each document uses numbered clauses (`Section N.M`) with YAML frontmatter
(`doc_id`, `doc_type`, `title`, `owner_role`, `effective_date`), which the
ingestion module preserves as chunk metadata for clause-level citation.

No real patient, staff, or Virtusa-confidential data is used anywhere in
this repository (Synthetic-Data Rule).

## 4. Domain Guardrails

1. **Citation-required.** Every non-abstaining answer must carry at least
   one `(document, clause_id)` citation drawn from retrieved context.
2. **Abstention behavior.** When the corpus does not support a confident
   answer, the assistant abstains (`grounded=false`, `abstained=true`)
   instead of fabricating a response. Enforced at two independent layers:
   the generation prompt (AC-05) and a deterministic code-level score
   threshold (`src/generation/generator.py::_code_level_abstention_check`).
3. **Reference-only, no advice.** The assistant surfaces the applicable
   policy/SOP text with citations; it must never issue a definitive legal
   or clinical decision beyond what the retrieved clause states.
4. **Out-of-scope handling.** Questions unrelated to the ingested policy
   corpus (e.g., facilities/parking, clinical dosing not present in the
   corpus) are treated identically to insufficient-evidence questions and
   trigger abstention. See `eval/golden_set.json` items `G-21`/`G-22`.

## 5. Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| Context precision (RAGAS) | ≥ 0.70 | `eval/run_eval.py` → `eval/reports/ragas_report.json` |
| Context recall (RAGAS) | ≥ 0.70 | same |
| Faithfulness (RAGAS) | ≥ 0.80 | same |
| Answer relevancy (RAGAS) | ≥ 0.75 | same |
| Retrieval hit rate (deterministic) | ≥ 0.85 | `eval/run_eval.py`, Python-only |
| Citation validity rate (deterministic) | 1.00 | same — every citation must reference a real `(doc_id, clause_id)` pair |
| Abstention accuracy (deterministic) | ≥ 0.90 | same, evaluated against `expect_abstain` labels in the golden set |

Targets are thresholds for the *project's own regression tracking*; they
are not the AAIE_011_HLC grading rubric itself (see Section 9 of the
rubric PDF for scoring).
