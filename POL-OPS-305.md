# Visitor Management Policy

## 2.1 Standard Visiting Hours

Standard visiting hours are 08:00 to 20:00. Immediate family may be present outside these hours for patients in critical condition, subject to unit-level approval.

## 2.2 Isolation Room Visitors

Visitors to isolation rooms must don the same PPE required for staff, as defined in SOP-IC-002, and are limited to two visitors at a time.

## 2.3 Responsible Role

The unit charge nurse approves visitor exceptions outside standard hours.
