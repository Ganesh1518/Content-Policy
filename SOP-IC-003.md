# Isolation Precautions SOP

## 2.1 Precaution Categories

Three precaution categories apply: Contact, Droplet, and Airborne. Each category has a distinct signage color and PPE requirement posted at the room entrance.

## 2.2 Contact Precautions

Used for multidrug-resistant organisms (MDRO). Requires gown and gloves for all room entry; dedicated or disposable equipment is required.

## 2.3 Droplet Precautions

Used for pathogens transmitted via respiratory droplets. Requires a surgical mask within 1 meter of the patient and closed doors are not required.

## 2.4 Airborne Precautions

Used for pathogens capable of long-distance airborne spread. Requires a negative-pressure room and a fit-tested N95/FFP2 respirator for all entrants.

## 2.5 Duration

Precautions remain in effect until the attending physician documents clinical resolution or until two consecutive negative test results are recorded, per the organism-specific protocol maintained by Infection Control.
