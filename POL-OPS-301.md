# Patient Admission and Bed Assignment Policy

## 2.1 Bed Assignment Priority

Bed assignment prioritizes clinical acuity first, then infection-control isolation needs, then length-of-stay optimization.

## 2.2 Responsible Role

The house supervisor makes final bed-assignment decisions during off-hours; the admitting charge nurse decides during regular shifts.
