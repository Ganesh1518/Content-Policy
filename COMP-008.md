# Hospital Compliance Manual — Staff Credentialing and Training

## 2.1 Initial Credentialing

Clinical staff may not provide unsupervised patient care until primary-source verification of license and required competencies is complete.

## 2.2 Annual Competency Review

All clinical staff must complete annual competency review for hand hygiene, PPE use, and fall-prevention protocols; non-completion within 30 days of the due date suspends clinical scheduling privileges.

## 2.3 Responsible Role

Unit managers verify staff training completion; the Compliance Officer audits completion rates quarterly.
