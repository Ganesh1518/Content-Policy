# Interpreter and Language Access Policy

## 2.1 Qualified Interpreter Requirement

For any consent discussion or significant clinical conversation with a patient who has limited English proficiency, a qualified medical interpreter (in-person or via approved video/phone service) must be used. Family members may not interpret for consent discussions.

## 2.2 Responsible Role

The treating clinician requests interpreter services; the unit clerk arranges access within 15 minutes for urgent situations.
